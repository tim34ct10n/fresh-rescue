Why it matters

Good food shouldn’t go to waste.

Fresh produce doesn’t all ripen at the same speed, so some of it reaches the end of its best quality before it’s sold. Fresh Rescue spots that produce early and offers it to you at a lower price, so it gets eaten instead of thrown away.

How it works

Three steps from shelf to your kitchen

  1. 1

    The store keeps an eye on its produce

    Sensors and shelf cameras check the condition of each batch of fresh produce, all day long.

  2. 2

    Good food that needs selling soon goes on rescue

    When produce is still good but should be sold soon, it appears in the Fresh Rescue section at a lower price.

  3. 3

    You tap “Rescue this”

    Pick the produce you want and take home good food for less, before it goes to waste.

FAQ

Questions shoppers ask

What is Fresh Rescue?

Fresh Rescue is a section of your supermarket’s website. It shows fresh produce that is still good but needs to be sold soon, at a lower price.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Just visit the website. There is no app to download and no sign-up needed to see the deals.

Why does the price change?

The price follows the produce’s quality score. As the score drops, the discount grows from 20% to 35% to 50%, so the food is more likely to be sold before it goes to waste.

Is rescue produce still good to eat?

The quality score is an estimate that helps you choose. It is not a use-by date, and the store’s normal food-safety checks still apply. Produce with a low score is removed from Fresh Rescue automatically.

Which products are included?

Fresh produce. Our prototype starts with Royal Gala apples and Cavendish bananas.
